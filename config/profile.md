# Who I am and what I want to read

I'm an ML engineer / researcher who wants a daily reading habit instead of a growing pile of tabs.

## Main focus (weighted highest)
- Large language models and agents: post-training (SFT, RLHF, RLVR, DPO and friends), reasoning, tool use, multi-agent systems, agent evaluation, long-context and memory.

## Second focus
- Computer vision and multimodal models: vision-language models, generative image and video models, video understanding, visual grounding.

## I care about
- Papers with a clear mechanism or ablation that explains *why* something works, not just a leaderboard.
- Methods I could actually try on a single GPU or via an API.
- Foundational papers that later work keeps citing.

## I care less about
- Pure benchmark papers with no new idea.
- Hardware-specific kernel work.
