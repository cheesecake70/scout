## What I understood

instead of fine tuning a model for a specific task or giving it a lot of guardrails policies and rules
they directly ask the model (like opus, jev or laya) to do the task
tasks like microservice autoscaling, dram sizing and video encoding
making individual resource-management decisions may be considerably simpler than developing a general strategy for making them

## Gaps

they say that if with say x amount of information a human can make a decision, then an llm can
and if the information is not enough for the human to make a decision, it probably isnt enough for an llm
